Add it in the app

Paid plans and the add-on are billed through Stripe on the web, and changes sync to the app automatically. Campaign rewards and platform fees always come out of your prepaid campaign wallet — never a subscription.

Flavorites Ads are in pilot — ad billing is currently off, so placements cost nothing today. When billing launches: $300 in intro ad credit with your first ad, and $100/month in bonus ad funds whenever you spend $300+ on ads.

What a $200 campaign actually buys

Say you run a casual spot and set an $8 reward — $9.20 all-in per visit with the free plan's fee. You top up your campaign wallet with $200. That's up to 21 verified customer visits — each one a real person who ordered food at your restaurant, confirmed by your own staff. If those guests average a $40 check, your wallet drove roughly $840 in orders at a fixed, known cost per customer.

Compare that with click-based advertising, where the same $200 buys taps on a screen and you never learn how many became customers.

Your spend is capped by design

  • Campaigns are prepaid — the wallet is the budget, and it can't go negative.
  • A campaign can't activate unless the wallet covers the next reward and fee.
  • Campaigns pause automatically when the balance drops below the next reward and fee.
  • Every wallet movement is backed by a double-entry ledger you can audit in the app.
