N° 02How campaigns work

Think about the result, not the settings.

Claim your restaurant once — after that, a campaign takes minutes to set up.

  1. Set your campaign

    Choose your budget, the reward per successful referral, a minimum qualifying spend, and whether it's for first-time customers or any qualifying guest.

  2. Let people spread the word

    Customers, creators, and advocates who genuinely love your restaurant share it — no outreach, no negotiated deals. Whoever sends the most customers earns the most.

  3. A customer visits and spends

    Your staff scans the referred customer's QR code and enters the order subtotal — the visit is verified in person, at your counter, on your terms.

  4. Pay for the result

    The referrer earns the reward, and you pay for a measurable customer visit — not speculative exposure. No qualifying visits, no spend.

N° 03Budget & control

You control the economics.

You decide what a successful referral is worth: the reward, the minimum spend that qualifies, whether it's first-timers only or any qualifying guest, and who's allowed to refer — every fan, referrers above a follower minimum, or a private list you invite. Pause or stop a campaign whenever you like.

  • A.
    Prepaid and capped by design.

    A campaign can never spend more than its budget, and it pauses itself when the balance can't cover the next reward. Unspent budget stays in your wallet — it isn't burned on exposure.

  • B.
    A window to dispute anything.

    Every reward stays pending after verification. If a redemption looks wrong, dispute it before a cent moves.

  • C.
    Every cent, accounted for.

    Top-ups, reserved rewards, reversals — each is a balanced double-entry ledger transaction you can audit in the app. Fraud controls are standard: one-time QR passes, no self-referrals, one reward per table.

N° 06Common questions

Restaurant owners ask us…

What counts as a successful referral?

You decide. A referral succeeds when the referred customer shows up, your staff scans their one-time QR code, and the order meets the minimum spend you set. You can also limit rewards to first-time customers only — or open them to any qualifying guest.

What happens if nobody gets referred?

You pay nothing for the attempt. Campaigns are performance-based: rewards only leave your prepaid budget when a qualifying visit is verified, and unspent budget stays in your wallet for the next campaign.

Who can refer customers to my restaurant?

Only Flavorites users who have saved your restaurant as one of their Flavorites — genuine fans, from regulars to creators. You can additionally require a minimum follower count or restrict referrals to a private list you invite.
